# Icon style

## Voice

The storybook at bedtime: glowing yellow crayon lines on a deep night-blue page, soft light-blue fills like moonlight, and tiny sparkle dots that make every object feel a little magical. Calm, dreamy and wondrous; the "tale" half of ScribbleTale, where drawings come alive.

## Drawing rules

1. The line is the hero and carries the full silhouette; plane is a translucent moonlight fill inside it.
2. Each icon gets one to three sparkle dots (1.5–2 unit discs or four-point twinkles) in accent, placed outside the object near a corner.
3. Sparkles sit in the keyline margin's inner edge and never overlap the object's line.
4. Corners use `sheet` and `box`; glowing things (smoke, star, magnet) use `glow` for extra softness.
5. Interior marks are line strokes, 2 to 5 per icon, kept 2.5 apart.
6. Objects sit level and centred; calm composition, no tilts beyond 15°.
7. In the inline tone the colours invert to navy line on white; sparkles turn gold.

## Vocabulary

- House with a lit window and a curling smoke line: home, glowing like a nightlight.
- Fat crayon with a sparkle at its tip: new-sketch, the magic starts here.
- Scrapbook with a doodle drifting out of it: my-drawings.
- Open picture book with twinkles rising from the pages: storybook, the signature icon.
- Page with a star half filled in moonlight: coloring-pages.
- Open crayon box with tips catching light: crayon-box.
- Star sticker peeling up at one point: stickers.
- Block eraser with a little swoosh of dots where it rubbed: eraser.
- Fridge door with a drawing held by a glowing magnet: fridge-gallery.
- Padlock with a key, no sparkles: grown-ups, deliberately calm and plain.

## Never

- Spooky or dark mood; night here means cosy, never scary.
- Neon glow filters, blurs or outer-glow effects; glow is implied by colour and sparkles only.
- Sparkles on the grown-ups padlock.
- More than three sparkles in one icon.
- Moons, owls or bedtime clichés replacing the real objects from the navigation.
